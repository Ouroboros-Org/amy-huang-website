Over the years, I've gathered pieces from martial arts, acrobatics, dance, and
flow. When I eventually found fire flow, something clicked. It felt like all
those pieces had been waiting to come together, giving me a new way to express
what I'd been building.

Today, I teach and perform, sharing these practices while continuing to explore
where they can take me.

Movement has been a way of exploring the world, and myself, for as long as I can
remember.

From martial arts to dance, from fire to stillness, I'm continually drawn to what
the body can discover, express, and become.

My practice is an ongoing journey of learning, experimenting, and sharing what I
find along the way.
